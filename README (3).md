# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard — Power BI

### Turning employee data into actionable HR insights

## 🔎 Project Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive **Microsoft Power BI** project designed to analyze employee data and understand patterns related to **employee attrition, demographics, job roles, salary levels, job satisfaction, and experience**.

The dashboard converts HR data into visual insights that can help HR teams identify workforce patterns and understand factors associated with employee attrition.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions

This dashboard focuses on questions such as:

- How many employees are in the organization?
- How many employees have left the organization?
- What is the overall attrition rate?
- Which departments have higher attrition?
- How does attrition vary by gender?
- Which job roles experience higher attrition?
- How is attrition distributed across salary slabs?
- How does job satisfaction relate to attrition?
- How does employee experience relate to attrition?

---

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

The Power BI dashboard includes the following analysis:

### 1️⃣ Workforce KPIs

Key metrics provide a quick overview of the workforce, including:

- **Total Employees**
- **Attrition Count**
- **Attrition Rate**
- **Average Experience**

### 2️⃣ Attrition by Department

A department-level analysis helps identify which departments contribute more to employee attrition.

### 3️⃣ Attrition by Gender

The dashboard compares attrition across different gender groups to identify workforce patterns.

### 4️⃣ Attrition by Job Role & Job Satisfaction

This analysis combines **Job Role** and **Job Satisfaction** to understand how satisfaction levels vary across roles and how they relate to employee attrition.

### 5️⃣ Attrition by Salary Slab

Employees are analyzed across salary slabs to identify whether attrition patterns differ across compensation levels.

### 6️⃣ Attrition by Experience

Employee experience is analyzed to understand how attrition changes across different levels of total professional experience.

---

## 🧠 Key HR Insights

The dashboard is designed to help HR stakeholders move from raw employee data to meaningful questions and decisions.

Potential business applications include:

- Identifying departments with higher attrition
- Understanding employee groups that may require further investigation
- Exploring the relationship between job satisfaction and attrition
- Evaluating attrition patterns across salary levels
- Understanding how experience is associated with employee retention
- Supporting data-driven workforce planning

> **Important:** The dashboard describes patterns in the underlying dataset. Relationships shown in the dashboard should not automatically be interpreted as causal relationships.

## 🗂️ Data Model

The Power BI report uses an HR analytics table containing fields including:

- `EmpID`
- `Age`
- `AgeGroup`
- `Attrition`
- `Attrition Count`
- `Attrition Rate%`
- `Department`
- `Gender`
- `JobRole`
- `JobSatisfaction`
- `SalarySlab`
- `TotalExperience(Years)`
- `YearsatCompany`

These fields are used to create the dashboard's KPIs, visualizations, filters, and analytical views.
